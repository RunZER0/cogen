# Cogen jurisdiction model

Cogen is jurisdiction-aware, not country-hard-coded. A Venture Twin carries explicit country, subdivision, locality, currency and locale context. Monetary assumptions use the venture currency dynamically. Regulatory research must resolve the governing national and subnational authorities from the venture jurisdiction and use current official sources.

A precise location may be used by the agent to infer jurisdiction, but inferred values must remain visible and correctable. Ambiguous locations are an intake gap rather than a licence to guess.
